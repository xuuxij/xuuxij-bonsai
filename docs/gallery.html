Each form is shown in cloud foliage and smooth bark with the planter it drew; each foliage on the same informal upright and each bark on the same formal upright; each planter under the same informal upright; each cloud at full strength.

Sky

Clouds drift behind the tree, drawn only where nothing else is: real cloud kinds at their real heights (rows as a share of the screen, from the top), one wind for the whole run, a mood per tree (clear, fair, high or misty). Each enters by the upwind edge or forms in place and dissolves. Two sets: the ink clouds, drawn outlines in three greys, the clouds of the black sky; and the tonal clouds, shaded masses in four greys at three depths (far ones pass behind the summits), the default over the landscape.
